# Table 1. Input datasets

**Table 1. Input statistical, demographic, remote-sensing and geospatial datasets used to generate the 1 km rural-income product.** Native raster and vector inputs were harmonised to the common 1 km analysis grid. County-level income labels were compiled by the authors from provincial statistical yearbooks; purchased administrative boundaries remain restricted and are not redistributed. No third-party raw input dataset is bundled in the public-release package.

| Dataset / product | Provider | Period and native resolution | Role in workflow | Access, licence and citation | Redistribution in the public package |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| County-level rural income labels | Compiled by the authors from provincial statistical yearbooks and officially published local statistical materials | 2019–2023; county-year records | Weak-supervision targets for model fitting and county-level validation | Cite the exact yearbook edition, table and page/source mapping; provenance crosswalk pending completion | Author-compiled raw table and reconstructable residuals are withheld until the yearbook-source crosswalk is complete |
| Provincial rural residents' per capita disposable income | National Bureau of Statistics of China, National Data | 2019–2023; province-year statistics | Province-year calibration constraint | Official National Data table; cite the National Bureau of Statistics and access date (Ref. 7) | No raw workbook; derived deflated values and calibration factors only |
| Provincial Consumer Price Index (previous year = 100) | National Bureau of Statistics of China, National Data | 2000–2024; 2019–2023 used; province-year statistics | Deflation of nominal income to 2019 constant yuan | Official National Data table; cite the National Bureau of Statistics and access date (Ref. 8) | No raw workbook; derived price indices and transformation metadata only |
| WorldPop Global 2 constrained population, R2025A UA v1 | WorldPop, University of Southampton | 2019–2023; 1 km annual rasters | Rural-population weights and county/province aggregation | CC BY 4.0; DOI: 10.5258/SOTON/WP00840 (Ref. 9) | No raw input in this release; derived outputs retain attribution |

Notes:

1. All annual covariates were resampled or aggregated to the common 1 km grid before model fitting. Static layers were held constant across 2019–2023.
2. National Bureau of Statistics source paths are: *Regional data → Provincial annual data → People's livelihood → Per capita disposable income → Rural residents' per capita disposable income (yuan)*, and *Price indices → Consumer Price Index (previous year = 100)*. The downloaded Excel workbooks are excluded until raw-table redistribution terms are confirmed.
3. WorldPop population layers are licensed under CC BY 4.0. GHS-SMOD reuse requires acknowledgement. VIIRS EOG data are public domain. MODIS products have no subsequent-use or redistribution restriction. WorldCover is CC BY 4.0. ERA5-Land and Sentinel-2 require Copernicus acknowledgement. The post-period OpenStreetMap road-density covariate used in an earlier experiment is excluded from the final v1.5 workflow.
4. The county-income row remains provenance-pending until each province-year source is linked to an exact statistical yearbook edition, table and page/URL. The administrative-boundary row remains restricted/pending until its purchased source, version, citation and publication permission are confirmed.

Source inventories: `metadata/source_data_inventory_public_sources_filled_draft.csv` and `metadata/source_data_inventory_scientific_data_draft.csv` in the frozen public-release package.
